## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Hiragana TFB** is a stylish decorative TrueType font designed for Windows, offering a unique aesthetic inspired by Japanese calligraphy. This font transforms Latin characters into visually engaging, brush-stroke styled glyphs, making it perfect for graphic designers, digital artists, and anyone looking to add a distinct flair to their projects. The **latest version** of Hiragana TFB is equipped with a complete A-Z mapping, allowing for both uppercase and lowercase variants.

With its quick installation and compatibility with various applications, Hiragana TFB integrates seamlessly into your design workflows. This font is ideal for headings and display text, providing a decorative touch without compromising legibility. While it does not support Japanese input, its artistic design effectively conveys a Japanese aesthetic.
